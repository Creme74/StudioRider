StudioRider @VERSION@

Lets someone listen to what your DAW plays, in a web browser: lossless FLAC, or Opus for slow connections. Free, no server to rent.

This installs, for all users of this Mac:

  • the Audio Unit and VST3 plugins, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs;
  • cloudflared @CLOUDFLARED@ (Cloudflare), which creates the public link. The plugin can update it later (Check for update at the bottom of its window).

An uninstaller is put in Applications > StudioRider.

Copies of StudioRider (or of its former names, StudioWire and MixShare) in your own ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins folder are replaced by this one.
