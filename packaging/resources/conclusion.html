StudioWire is installed

  1. In your DAW, insert StudioWire last on the master track (as an Audio Unit or a VST3 plugin).
  2. Pick the Stream quality, click Start sharing.
  3. Copy the link and send it to the listener.

If your DAW was open, quit and reopen it. If StudioWire does not show up, make your DAW rescan its plugins (in its plugin settings).

To uninstall: Applications > StudioWire > Uninstall StudioWire.
